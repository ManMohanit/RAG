SelfAI Sample Interview Guide

Eligibility requirements
Candidates should have a basic understanding of Python, SQL, data structures, and object-oriented programming. Freshers are welcome. A portfolio or two small personal projects is recommended.

Tell me about yourself
Use a three-part structure: present role or education, two relevant strengths or projects, and why you want the specific opportunity. Keep the response between 60 and 90 seconds. Avoid giving your full life story.

Interview preparation
Read the job description, prepare one STAR-format example for teamwork and one for problem solving, and practise explaining a project you built. Prepare two thoughtful questions for the interviewer.

STAR method
STAR means Situation, Task, Action, and Result. Start by briefly explaining the situation and your responsibility. Describe the actions you personally took. Finish with a measurable or clear result.

Follow-up after an interview
Send a short thank-you email within 24 hours. Mention one useful part of the discussion and restate your interest in the role.
